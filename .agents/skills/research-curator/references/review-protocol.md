# Research review protocol

## Scope review

- Does the work answer the stated question?
- Did it stay inside the task boundary?
- Will the output change reasoning, routing, terminology, or an eval?

## Evidence review

- Are primary or original sources investigated where practical?
- Are locators precise and verified?
- Are relevance, method, recency, incentives, and limitations explicit?
- Are conflicting findings preserved?
- Does claim confidence match the weakest material evidence?

## Knowledge review

- Were existing IDs and aliases searched?
- Is each proposed entity canonical rather than duplicated prose?
- Is `primary_domain` ownership clear and are facets used for overlap?
- Are relationships directional and supported?
- Are deprecated or conflicting entities handled explicitly?

## Evaluation review

- Does the proposed behavior follow from the evidence?
- Can an eval observe the intended behavior without requiring one exact answer?
- Are hard failures truly harmful rather than stylistic preferences?
- Do multilingual variants preserve the same reasoning expectations?

## Publication decision

Approve only when acceptance criteria and schema validation pass. Otherwise return to draft with specific gaps. Rejection preserves rationale and provenance.
