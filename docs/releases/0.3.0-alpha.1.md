# ProductOS 0.3.0-alpha.1

**Date:** 2026-09-27

## Outcome

This alpha turns the Phase 0 research design into an executable content pipeline and proves it with one bounded knowledge slice.

## Delivered

- Research Curator skill with entity-extraction and review protocols;
- canonical per-entity source, claim, concept, framework, terminology, decision-pattern, relationship, anti-pattern, case, and playbook directories;
- additional anti-pattern, case, and playbook schemas;
- cross-reference and filename validation;
- deterministic knowledge index generation;
- deterministic concept and terminology collision checks;
- research task, review notes, synthesis, canonical entities, and eval for decision quality versus outcome quality.

## Evidence slice

The slice uses:

- Baron and Hershey's original 1988 outcome-bias experiments;
- Aiyer et al.'s 2023 preregistered replication and extension;
- the Society of Decision Professionals' current core principles as professional normative guidance.

The synthesis distinguishes evaluation of what was knowable at commitment from learning that becomes possible after observing outcomes.

## Validation

- all schemas, fixtures, registries, and cross-references pass;
- the generated index is current;
- deterministic duplicate checks pass;
- both repository skills pass the official skill quick validator;
- unit tests and Ruff pass;
- all three registered source URLs were checked, with the accessible archived university-hosted copy used for the 1988 paper.

## Not yet satisfied

- Extracted entities remain `in_review` pending independent editorial verification.
- No direct Product Management study of outcome bias was included in this bounded slice.
- Behavioral eval scoring remains unconfigured.

## Recommended next gate

Obtain independent review of the slice and calibrate the behavior-eval protocol. After those gates, begin Wave 1 as separate research tasks rather than a single broad research instruction.
