# Capability taxonomy

ProductOS organizes knowledge through facets rather than a single flat list.

## Facets

- **Capabilities:** judgment and reasoning abilities.
- **Practices:** repeatable Product Management activities and methods.
- **Contexts:** business and market settings that change how reasoning applies.
- **Product types:** technical or economic product archetypes.
- **Roles and interfaces:** organizational responsibilities and collaboration boundaries.
- **Constraints:** risk, responsibility, regulatory, and operating conditions.

## Ownership rule

Every entity declares one `primary_domain`. Other applicable areas are expressed as facets. If two domains appear to own the same concept, reviewers choose the domain responsible for its definition and link the other domain through facets or relationships.

## Initial mapping examples

| Specification topic | Primary facet |
|---|---|
| Decision Making | capability |
| Product Discovery | practice |
| Enterprise Product Management | context |
| Platform Products | product type |
| Product Marketing / Sales Interface | role or interface |
| Privacy | constraint |

The full controlled vocabulary will be populated during research infrastructure work. Phase 0 defines only the model and validation shape.
