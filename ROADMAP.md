# ProductOS Roadmap

The roadmap uses quality gates rather than content-volume quotas. Framework and eval counts are indicators, not definitions of success.

## 0.1.0 — Architecture and bootstrap

**Status:** Complete

Deliver:

- repository and governance files;
- architecture decisions and capability facets;
- research, source-quality, and terminology models;
- versioned schemas and fixtures;
- deterministic repository validation;
- eval strategy and rubric skeleton;
- continuous integration.

Exit gate:

- all schemas are valid;
- valid fixtures pass and invalid fixtures fail;
- root documentation is internally consistent;
- no Product Management domain knowledge has been populated.

## 0.2.0 — Reasoning foundation and thin vertical slice

**Status:** Alpha implemented; scored behavior baseline pending

Deliver:

- evidence, uncertainty, risk, and decision models;
- Product Reasoning Engine v0;
- 10–15 seed decision evals;
- a narrow Staff PM skill covering three to five representative decisions;
- a recorded baseline against the eval suite.

The Staff PM integration is intentionally moved earlier than in the original specification so architectural problems surface before broad research.

## 0.3.0 — Research infrastructure

**Status:** Alpha implemented; first knowledge slice awaiting independent review

Deliver:

- source, claim, concept, framework, terminology, and relationship registries;
- Research Curator skill;
- review and freshness workflow;
- generated indexes;
- one complete source-to-eval research slice.

## 0.4.0 — Product judgment alpha

Research and integrate:

- Product Sense and Judgment;
- Decision Making;
- Systems Thinking;
- Product Strategy;
- Customer Understanding.

Release the first Staff PM Alpha after regression and multilingual smoke tests.

## 0.5.0–0.9.0 — Incremental capability waves

Expand through reusable clusters:

1. discovery, research, UX, and behavioral science;
2. metrics, analytics, statistics, experimentation, and causal reasoning;
3. goals, planning, roadmaps, prioritization, and portfolio;
4. growth, Product Marketing, GTM, pricing, packaging, business models, and economics;
5. technical, platform, API, data, developer, AI, and modernization contexts;
6. operating model, organization, leadership, and transformation;
7. privacy, security, accessibility, trust and safety, ethics, and regulation.

Every wave follows the same loop:

```text
bounded research tasks
→ reviewed sources and claims
→ canonical knowledge
→ skill routing
→ new evals
→ full regression
```

## 0.10.0 — Evaluation expansion

- broaden decision-pattern and multilingual coverage;
- establish holdout cases;
- calibrate automated judges against human review;
- analyze regressions and failure clusters.

## 0.11.0 — Release candidate

- harden skill routing and progressive disclosure;
- resolve high-severity failure modes;
- complete installation and contributor documentation;
- verify the target runtime end to end.

## 1.0.0 — Staff Product Manager stable

Release only when core scenarios demonstrate consistent decision quality, evidence discipline, multilingual fidelity, and regression stability.
