# Research workflow

Research is organized as bounded tasks rather than broad collection.

```text
backlog → active → reviews → completed → synthesis → canonical knowledge
```

- `backlog/`: accepted research contracts waiting to start.
- `active/`: work in progress with evidence notes.
- `reviews/`: source, method, contradiction, and synthesis review.
- `completed/`: reviewed research task packages.
- `synthesis/`: narrative cross-task synthesis that links to canonical entities.

See [methodology.md](methodology.md) for acceptance and review rules.
